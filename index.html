How to Play

  • Divide into two teams.
  • One player acts as the clue-giver. They must describe the Target Word to their team without using any of the Taboo words below it.
  • +1 point for correct guesses.
  • -1 point if you accidentally say a Taboo word.
  • 0 points to skip a hard word.
  • When time runs out, pass the device to the other team!
