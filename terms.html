2. Description of Service

Taboosey is a free, browser-based party word game that allows players to enjoy a Taboo-style word-guessing experience without physical cards. The service includes:

  • A built-in word deck spanning multiple categories (Food & Drink, Animals, Technology, Nature, Places, and Actions)
  • A Custom (AI) category that generates game cards based on user-supplied topics using third-party AI services
  • Score tracking, round history, and game management features
  • A Progressive Web App (PWA) that can be installed to a device home screen
