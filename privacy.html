Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Taboosey. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may share with us.

2. Data Collection

Taboosey is designed to run locally in your web browser. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not actively collect personal identity information such as names or email addresses unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

3. Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Please be aware of the following:

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings. (Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info).

4. Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how players use Taboosey — for example, which categories are most popular and how many games are started. Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated; it does not identify individual users. Among the events we track is the topic entered for the Custom (AI) Category feature. Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as names, medical conditions, or financial details) as a custom AI topic, as this text may be logged as an analytics event.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at feedback@taboosey.com.
